Just Make Bread — Stage 1 worker v008

This runtime candidate contains the user-authored worker mesh/design and the
user-approved v008 block-manipulation animation derivative. It exposes the
exact Idle_A, Walking_A, Holding_A, CarryWalking_A, Running_A, BlockPush_A,
BlockPull_A, and BlockMoveFront_A actions. The animations are in-place;
authoritative gameplay owns player and production-block position, collision,
input, and lifecycle state.

The v008 derivative changes only the three block-manipulation clips to a
24-frame alternating stump-step loop. Its stepping phase is presentation-only
and follows accepted production-block X displacement; it does not add run or
dash gameplay, root motion, or a new gameplay timing rule. The user-authored
mesh/design and animation derivative are used with permission for this project.

No authoring paths, private metadata, provider credentials, source archives, or
source textures are part of the public runtime payload. Running_A remains
validation-only. The prior v007, v005, and separate KayKit-based v002 assets
remain versioned rollback/fallback paths with their own provenance sidecars.
